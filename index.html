01 • Problem

What Is the Problem?

Hostel mess cooks food based on rough guesswork because daily student attendance keeps changing.

The Problem Today

  • Fixed Cooking Guesswork: Kitchen staff cook roughly the same large batch every meal regardless of actual turnout.
  • Changing Student Attendance: Weekends, exams, and popular vs unpopular dishes cause student count to rise and fall unpredictably.
  • Extra Food Wasted: Cooked food cannot be stored for long, so all surplus food gets thrown away.

What Our Project Does

  • Predicts Expected Eaters: Estimates student attendance using the day of the week, exams, and menu items.
  • Suggests Practical Cooking Pots: Converts expected eaters into actual pot and tray counts for the cooks.
  • Includes a Safe Buffer: Always adds a small buffer so the kitchen never runs out of food.
  • Uses Real Mess Logs: We are recording daily plate counts and leftover data from the hostel mess to validate this.
What the Mess Manager Told Us

“We don't need a complicated student voting app. Just tell us how many pots to cook before we light the stove.”

02 • Scope

What We Will Build

Project scope and deliverables for this semester.

Core Deliverables

  • Kitchen Web App: Clean interface for the mess manager and cooks to check daily cooking recommendations.
  • Attendance Prediction Model: Estimates how many students will eat based on historical patterns, weekdays, and exams.
  • Dish Quantity Calculator: Translates student attendance into exact cooking pots, liters of dal, and trays of chapatis.
  • Shortage Safety Buffer: Automatically adds safe margins so students never face food shortages.
  • Configurable for Any Mess: Easily adjustable for any hostel menu, timing, and pot capacities.
  • Offline Support: Continues functioning even if mess kitchen Wi-Fi is temporarily unavailable.

05 • Core Logic

How the Prediction Works

4 simple steps to calculate how much food to prepare each meal.

Step 1: Predict Attendance

Estimates how many students will show up using the day of the week, meal time, and exam schedules.

Step 2: Check Dish Demand

Uses past mess records to see how popular today's dishes are so popular items get more preparation.

Step 3: Measure in Kitchen Units

Converts student numbers into clear, practical kitchen measurements: kg for gravies, Liters for dal, and Trays for chapatis.

Step 4: Add Safety Buffer

Always includes a safe extra buffer so the kitchen never runs short during meal service.

Configurable per Hostel: Easily adaptable to any hostel's menu items, meal timings, and kitchen equipment.

07 • Quality

Speed, Privacy & Reliability

Ensuring the tool is fast, secure, and always dependable during kitchen operations.

Fast & Available

  • Instant Results: Cooking advice generates immediately upon meal selection.
  • Offline Operation: Functions completely offline when kitchen Wi-Fi drops.
  • Automatic Sync: Saves and syncs outcome logs once internet is re-established.
  • High Uptime: Always ready when kitchen preparation begins.

Privacy & Permissions

  • Zero Personal Data: Only aggregate plate counts and food weights are logged. No names or roll numbers.
  • Role Permissions:
    • Manager: Can adjust daily batch recommendations.
    • Cook: Views cooking pots and enters leftover counts.
    • Warden: Views weekly summaries and waste reports.
  • Audit History: Logs any manual overrides for transparency.

Usability & Safety

  • Clear Interface: Large tap targets and clear text designed for kitchen environments.
  • Safe Fallback: Defaults to recent rolling averages if any inputs are missing.
  • Modular Structure: Separates calculation logic from UI display for easy maintenance.

Conclusion & References

How Mestimator improves upon current practices and the academic backing supporting our project.

Comparison Against Baseline Methods
Method How It Works Outcome
Current Status Quo Fixed intuition-based preparation Frequent overcooking and food waste
Same Day Last Week Repeat same day from previous week Better, but ignores exams and menu variations
Rolling Moving Average Average of recent historical days Smoother, but misses sudden calendar spikes
Mestimator (Ours) Feature-aware prediction + safe batch quantizer Reduced food waste & zero shortages

  • Prediction Accuracy: Closeness of forecasted attendance to actual plates served.
  • Waste Reduction: Measured reduction in surplus leftover food compared to baseline.
  • Service Reliability: Zero unmanaged food shortage events during meal service.
