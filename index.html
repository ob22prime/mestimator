02 • Problem Context

Introduction & Campus Context

Bridging the gap between static mess cooking rules and fluctuating student dining turnout.

The Campus Food Wastage Reality

  • Fixed Cooking Guesswork: Kitchens prepare fixed batches for ~800 residents based purely on intuition.
  • High Turnout Swings (25% to 50%): Attendance drops sharply on weekends and spikes during exams or popular dishes (Paneer vs. Lauki).
  • Perishable Cooked Food Discarded: Surplus cooked food cannot be safely stored across shifts and is discarded daily.

Presentation & Project Objectives

  • Dish-Level Batch Guidance: Suggest exact kg and liters per menu item, not just general headcount.
  • Active Campus Log: Collecting daily plate counts, leftover weights, and shortages with mess staff since August 2026.
  • Physical Kitchen Batches: Convert demand into realistic cooking vessels (standard deghs and trays) with safety margins.
  • Benchmarked Against Baselines: Evaluated against manager gut feeling, last week, and 7-day moving averages.
Key Finding from Mess Manager Discussion

“We don't need an open student voting app. We need simple, explainable daily batch numbers so the cook knows whether to prepare 45 kg or 65 kg before lighting the stove.”

03 • Scope & Boundaries

Project Scope & Deliverables

Clearly defined deliverables for the 17-week semester, with strict operational boundaries.

In-Scope Core Deliverables

  • Kitchen Web App (PWA): Fast, mobile-friendly interface for cooks to check batch quantities and log leftover weights.
  • Multi-Factor Demand Engine: Predicts diner attendance and dish splits using weekday, calendar events, and dish history.
  • Portion & Batch Recipe Optimizer: Converts predicted diners into cooking batches (kg of Rice, Liters of Dal, hot cases).
  • Safety Margin & Uncertainty Range: Shows confidence intervals (e.g., 520 ± 35 diners) to prevent shortages.
  • Multi-Mess Configuration: Easily customizable for different hostel menus, schedules, and vessel sizes.

Explicit Non-Goals (Out of Scope)

  • NO Medicine Sharing: Removed from original concept due to strict health and drug regulations.
  • NO Open Student Redistribution: Mess food remains institution-controlled per FSSAI safety norms.
  • NO Mandatory Student Voting: Student preferences are an optional signal, not a hard system dependency.
  • NO Automated Cooking Hardware: Advisory decision support only; staff always maintain final cooking control.
  • NO Black-Box LLM Wrapper: Forecasting uses statistical and machine learning models (XGBoost / Holt-Winters).

06 • Architecture

System Features & Decision Engine

A multi-stage decision model converting uncertain turnout into practical kitchen batches.

1. Turnout Forecasting Model

Estimates expected diner attendance based on key input variables:

Features: [Day of Week, Meal Shift, Academic Calendar, Weather, Prior Week Turnout]

Compares XGBoost vs. 7-Day Seasonal Moving Average.

2. Dish-Level Preference Matrix

Distributes predicted diners into individual dish demand using historical popularity weights:

Dish Demand = Expected Diners × Historical Dish Popularity Ratio

3. Recipe Yield & Batch Quantization

Converts diner demand into physical cooking vessels under batch constraints:

Prep Batch = (Diners × Portion Weight + 6.5% Buffer) rounded to Pot Size

Ensures food is cooked in realistic batch increments (e.g. 5kg pots or 20L kettles).

4. Asymmetric Shortage Guard

Running out of food is penalized heavily (3.5× penalty for shortage vs 1× for minor surplus):

Operational Penalty = Excess Waste Cost + 3.5 × (Shortage Penalty)
Multi-Mess SaaS Configuration: Configurable per hostel for meal timings, custom weekly menus, container capacities, and dish portion sizes.

08 • Non-Functional Requirements

Performance, Security & Quality Attributes

Engineered for rock-solid reliability, fast response times in the kitchen, and zero privacy compromise.

Performance Goals

  • Recommendation Latency: < 400 ms on mobile devices for batch calculations.
  • Offline Availability: 100% core calculations operate even if kitchen Wi-Fi drops.
  • Fast Data Sync: Background sync finishes within 3 seconds of network reconnect.
  • Uptime Target: 99.9% availability during active meal hours (6:00 AM to 9:00 PM).

Security & Privacy

  • Zero Student Privacy Risk: Only aggregate plate counts and food weights stored; zero student ID or biometric data.
  • Role-Based Access (RBAC):
    • Mess Manager: Full edit & override permissions.
    • Kitchen Staff: View batch sizes & enter leftovers.
    • Warden: Read-only weekly reports.
  • Audit Logging: Immutable log for every manual override with reason code.

Quality & Reliability

  • Kitchen Usability: Large tap targets (min 48px) and high-contrast color scheme.
  • Safe Fallback: If ML inputs are missing, system defaults to 14-day median rolling average.
  • Modular Codebase: Decoupled forecasting engine from UI and database layers.
